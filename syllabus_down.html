Catalog Description:
This course provides a practical approach to the study of digital and integrated devices. The course will include the study of logic gates, flip-flops, latches, counters, encoders/decoders, multiplexers, arithmetic circuits, oscillators, timers, analog-to-digital circuits, digital-to-analog circuits, solid-state memory, and operational amplifiers. In addition, the study of solid-state circuits and devices. The course will include the study of diodes, transistors, FETs, thyristors, and optoelectronic devices. Examination of common circuits will include power supplies, amplifiers, solid-state switches, and regulators will be introduced.

Course End Competencies

  • Demonstrate the proper operation of logic gates, counters, encoders/decoders, and arithmetic circuits.
  • Explain the operation of oscillators, timers, and operational amplifiers.
  • Explain the operation of flip-flops, latches, A-to-D, D-to-A circuits, solid-state memory, and multiplexers.
  • Demonstrate proper troubleshooting procedures and practices.
  • Demonstrate the proper operation of semiconductor devices.
  • Explain the operation of BJTs, UJT, FETs and thyristors.
  • Explain the operation of opto-electronic devices.
  • Identify operating parameters and component ratings of common electronic devices.
