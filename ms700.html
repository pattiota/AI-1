📋 Purpose of this Study Guide

▼

This study guide will help you understand what to expect on the AI-900 exam and includes a comprehensive summary of topics the exam covers, along with additional resources. The information and materials should help you focus your studies as you prepare for the certification.


Key Requirements:

  • ✅ A score of 700 or greater is required to pass
  • 📚 Study materials are available in multiple formats
  • 🔄 Certifications expire annually and can be renewed
  • 🌐 Available in multiple languages

📅 Exam Updates & Important Notes

▼
🔄 Regular Updates

Exams are updated periodically to reflect skills required for the role. English versions are updated first, with localized versions following approximately 8 weeks later.

🌍 Language Availability

If your preferred language isn't available, you can request an additional 30 minutes to complete the exam.

📋 Question Coverage

Most questions cover General Availability (GA) features. Preview features may be included if commonly used.

👥 Target Audience Profile

▼
🎯 Who Should Take This Exam

This exam demonstrates knowledge of machine learning and AI concepts and related Microsoft Azure services. Suitable for both technical and non-technical backgrounds.

📚 Prerequisites

Not Required: Data science and software engineering experience

Beneficial to have:

  • Basic cloud concepts
  • Client-server applications understanding
🚀 Career Path

Azure AI Fundamentals can help prepare for other Azure role-based certifications like Azure Data Scientist Associate or Azure AI Engineer Associate, but it's not a prerequisite.
