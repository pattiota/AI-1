🚀 Getting Started with AI in Your Courses

Ready to integrate AI tools? Start small, focus on solving specific teaching challenges, and gradually expand your AI toolkit based on what works best for your students and teaching style.

Implementation Roadmap

🎯 Week 1-2: Explore

Try 2-3 free AI tools relevant to your discipline. Start with ChatGPT, Google's NotebookLM, and one subject-specific tool. Spend 30 minutes exploring each.

📝 Week 3-4: Experiment

Use AI tools for lesson planning, quiz generation, or content creation. Document what works and what doesn't. Share experiences with colleagues.

👥 Week 5-6: Introduce to Students

Demonstrate AI tools to students. Establish guidelines for appropriate use. Create assignments that leverage AI while maintaining academic integrity.

🔄 Ongoing: Iterate & Improve

Regularly assess effectiveness. Stay updated on new tools. Join faculty AI communities. Continuously refine your AI integration strategy.

Best Practices for Faculty

✅ Do's

  • Start with free tools before investing in premium ones
  • Be transparent with students about AI use
  • Use AI to enhance, not replace, human interaction
  • Verify AI-generated content for accuracy
  • Establish clear AI usage policies
  • Focus on tools that solve real problems

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't rely on AI for all content creation
  • Don't ignore privacy and data concerns
  • Don't use AI without understanding how it works
  • Don't assume all AI outputs are accurate
  • Don't implement tools without training
  • Don't overwhelm students with too many tools
